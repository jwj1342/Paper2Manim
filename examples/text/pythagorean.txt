The Pythagorean theorem states that in any right-angled triangle, the square of the length of the hypotenuse (the side opposite the right angle) equals the sum of the squares of the lengths of the other two sides. Symbolically, if a and b are the legs and c is the hypotenuse, then a^2 + b^2 = c^2. Visualize this by drawing a right triangle, labelling each side, and showing the equation appearing alongside.
